# Exploratory Testing Ruling

**Ruling:** `CONDITIONAL | trigger: the change's linked issue is a spike or
research issue, named as such in its title or labels`

Unscripted probing in this repo takes the form of spikes: when work covers
new ground, a spike or research issue precedes the functionality, and the
exploration's findings are documented on that issue's thread rather than
in tracked files. When the trigger fires, done means the spike's probing
happened and its results are recorded on the issue.

Routine changes to established surfaces do not fire this layer.
