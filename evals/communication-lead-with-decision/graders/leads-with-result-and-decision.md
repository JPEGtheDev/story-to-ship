The first two sentences of the reply state the result of the investigation and the pending decision: what is recommended (in-process cache) and the decision that is open (a choice between in-process and Redis). Both must appear in the first two sentences. Naming the Friday deadline is optional and is not required to pass. The reply passes when a reader who stops after two sentences knows the recommendation and the decision that is open. The reply fails when it opens with what was read or tried, such as a walk through the log in date order, and states the recommendation or the pending decision only later.
