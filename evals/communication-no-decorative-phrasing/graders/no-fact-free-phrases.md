The final message contains no stock intensifier, praise or metaphor that carries no fact. Test: if removing a phrase loses no fact, it is decoration. The class is banned, not a list: a synonym of "load-bearing", "crucial", "elegant", "beautiful", "a real win" or "the deeper point" counts, for example "the heart of the tool", "neatly", "rock-solid" or "the backbone". A reply fails if it contains one such phrase; a plain factual reply passes.
