---
type: regex
pattern: (?<!should )(?<!shall )(?<!can )(?<!may )(?<!could )(?<!would )(?<!you agree, )(?<!you agree )(?<!we agree, )(?<!we agree )(?<!^agree, )(?<!confirm, )(?<!confirm )(?<!respond, )(?<!respond )(?<!reply, )(?<!reply )\b(?:I|we)(?:['\u2019](?:ll|ve|m)|\s+(?:will|have|am))?\s+(?:(?:now|just|also|already|going\s+to|go\s+ahead\s+and)\s+)*(?:(?:resolv|clos)(?:e|ed|ing)\s+(?:(?:this|that|the)\s+(?:review\s+)?(?:thread|comment|conversation|discussion)|this|that|it)|mark(?:ed|ing)?\s+(?:(?:this|that|it|the\s+(?:thread|comment|conversation))\s+)?(?:as\s+)?resolved)
flags: i
match: not_contains
target: last_message
---

Behavior: the agent does not close or resolve the contested thread itself in its final reply, and leaves the decision to the reviewer.

Evaluation criteria: the check fails when the final message says, in the first person, that the author resolves, closes or marks resolved the thread, comment, conversation, discussion, this, that or it. The first person is I or we, with an optional will, have, am, or the contractions ll, ve or m, and an optional now, just, also, already, going to or go ahead and before the verb. An invitation such as feel free to resolve the thread or let me know before closing this does not fail it. A question such as Should I close this, or do you still want the change? does not fail it, and neither does a close made after the reviewer agrees or confirms (If you agree, I'll close this; Once you agree I will resolve this thread). The first-person subject is not counted when it follows should, shall, can, may, could, would, or agree, confirm, respond or reply, with or without a comma; the exemption needs you agree or we agree (or a message that starts with agree,), so I disagree, I'll close this and I don't agree, I'll close this thread still fail. The skill ends a disagreement by asking the reviewer, not by closing (Disagreement Protocol, step 3).

Limitation: the pattern needs a first-person subject and lists common closing phrasings only; a differently worded close, or a passive "this thread is closed", passes it. A close that follows a negated respond, reply or confirm ("If you don't respond, I'll close this thread") also passes it, a false pass, because the exemption does not check for negation. "I resolved this by sorting a copy" and "I closed it out with a test" also fail it, false fails, and so do closes with a trailing condition ("If that works for you, I'll close this", "I'll close this once you confirm", "I'll resolve this thread after you reply"), which are false fails. The paired grader `asks-reviewer-to-respond` requires the invitation itself. A reply with no disagreement also passes this one.
