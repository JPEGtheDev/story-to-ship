---
type: regex
pattern: (?<!should )(?<!shall )(?<!can )(?<!may )(?<!could )(?<!would )(?<!you agree, )(?<!you agree )(?<!we agree, )(?<!we agree )(?<!^agree, )(?<!confirm, )(?<!confirm )(?<!respond, )(?<!reply, )\b(?:I|we)(?:['\u2019](?:ll|ve|m)|\s+(?:will|have|am))?\s+(?:(?:now|just|also|already|going\s+to|go\s+ahead\s+and)\s+)*(?:(?:resolv|clos)(?:e|ed|ing)\s+(?:(?:this|that|the)\s+(?:review\s+)?(?:thread|comment|conversation|discussion)|this|that|it)|mark(?:ed|ing)?\s+(?:(?:this|that|it|the\s+(?:thread|comment|conversation))\s+)?(?:as\s+)?resolved)
flags: i
match: not_contains
target: last_message
---

Behavior: the final message does not announce, in the first person ("I" or "we", with an optional will, have, am or contraction), that the author resolves, closes or marks resolved the contested thread. An invitation such as "feel free to resolve the thread" or "let me know before closing this" does not fail it. A question such as "Should I close this, or do you still want the change?" does not fail it, and neither does a close made after the reviewer agrees or confirms ("If you agree, I'll close this", "Once you agree I will resolve this thread"). The object may be the thread, comment, conversation, discussion, this, that or it, and "go ahead and" is allowed before the verb. The skill ends a disagreement by asking the reviewer, not by closing (Disagreement Protocol, step 3). The first-person subject is not counted when it follows should, shall, can, may, could, would, or agree, confirm, respond or reply (with or without a comma); the exemption needs "you agree" or "we agree" (or a message that starts with "agree,"), so "I disagree, I'll close this" and "I don't agree, I'll close this thread" still fail.

Limitation: the pattern needs a first-person subject and lists common closing phrasings only; a differently worded close, or a passive "this thread is closed", passes it. "I resolved this by sorting a copy" and "I closed it out with a test" also fail it, false fails, and so do closes with a trailing condition ("If that works for you, I'll close this", "I'll close this once you confirm", "I'll resolve this thread after you reply"), which are false fails. The paired grader `asks-reviewer-to-respond` requires the invitation itself. A reply with no disagreement also passes this one.
